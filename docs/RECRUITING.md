# Cohort recruiting — get the first 30–50 word-lovers (for the D7 test)

**Goal:** not virality — *behavior*. You need ~30–50 real people on TestFlight so you can read
**Day-7 unprompted return** and **share rate**. Hand-recruit; talk to people.

**Rules of the road (so you don't get banned / ignored):**
- Read each community's self-promo rules first. Many require a flair, a megathread, or mod approval.
- Lead with genuine love of words, not "check out my app." Ask for *honest* feedback, including
  "I downloaded it and never opened it again" — that's the data you need.
- Replace `[TESTFLIGHT_LINK]` everywhere. Add one screenshot/short screen-recording of the feed +
  the "claim a word → write why it's yours" moment.
- Track where each signup came from (so you learn which channel works).

## After they're in — what to watch (your only metrics now)
- **D7 return:** of people who open Day 1, how many open again on Day 7 *without* you nudging them?
  - `< ~10%` → it's a novelty toy → **stop / pivot** (don't build Phase 3).
  - `~20%+` → real signal → build the deepening features (self-portrait lexicon, resurfacing,
    feeling-collections).
- **Share rate:** does anyone send a word to a friend on their own?
- (Events already fire in the app: `app_open`, `word_claimed`, `note_added`, `card_shared` — once
  you wire a live analytics backend you'll read these directly.)

**Ask every tester one open question:** *"Did you open it again after the first day? Why / why not?"*
The answer is worth more than any metric.
