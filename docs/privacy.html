Privacy Policy

Verbum · Last updated 20 June 2026

Verbum is a vocabulary app. We built it to collect as little about you as possible. We run no advertising, no third-party trackers, and we do not sell or share your personal data.

What the app stores

  • On your device. The words you save to your lexicon, your personal notes, your streaks, learning progress, and settings are stored locally on your device.
  • iCloud (optional). If you are signed in to iCloud, this same data syncs through Apple's CloudKit to your own private iCloud account, so it follows you across your devices. It is stored in your iCloud, managed by Apple — we, the developer, cannot read it.
  • Sign in with Apple (optional). If you choose to sign in, we store the Apple user identifier (and the email address you choose to share) on your device's Keychain to link your synced data to you. You can use Verbum without signing in.
  • Purchases. Subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple. We never receive your card number or payment details.
  • Notifications. Daily word reminders are scheduled locally on your device. We do not run a push server.

What we do not do

  • No advertising and no ad identifiers.
  • No third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • We operate no servers that store your personal content, and we never sell or share your data.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to Settings → Delete Account to erase your local data and the copy stored in your private iCloud.

Children

Verbum is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
